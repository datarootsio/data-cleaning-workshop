# Workshop code of conduct

## Our commitment

Participants and facilitators aim to make the workshop welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming language and respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give constructive feedback on ideas and code; make room for questions and alternative approaches.
- Respect other participants' privacy and ask before sharing their work or personal information.
- Help others learn and accept feedback in good faith.

Harassment, sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, insults, personal attacks, intimidation, and
publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies during workshop sessions and in associated repository discussions and collaboration spaces.
Report concerns privately to a workshop facilitator through the session's contact channel. If the concern involves that
facilitator, contact another organizer. Facilitators will handle reports discreetly, share information only as needed to
address the concern, and respond fairly and proportionately.

Organizers may ask someone to stop harmful behavior, remove inappropriate contributions, or exclude a participant from
the workshop or repository spaces. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable.
